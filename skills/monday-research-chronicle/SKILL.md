# MONDAY Research Chronicle

Create durable research or build records for MONDAY. The canonical destination is:

`/Users/chris.binion/Knowledge Vault/Monday Knowledge/500 Research Journal`

## Scope and authority

- Use only work whose evidence is available in the request, explicitly authorized local artifacts, or cited authoritative sources. Treat inaccessible evidence as unknown, not absent.
- A research chronicle records what was verified, reported, inferred, decided, and still unresolved. It is not a status update, a personal journal, or a place to make outcomes sound more certain than they are.
- When a daily Captain's Log review identifies work that needs a durable technical or research record, use only the separately inventoried evidence and preserve the distinction between the reflective narrative and this source-bound chronicle.
- Do not write a durable entry until the proposed text has been reviewed and Chris explicitly approves saving it. A request to research, analyze, or draft is not approval to record a final chronicle.
- Do not import private journal material, Thermo Fisher project evidence, secrets, credentials, or raw private communications unless Chris explicitly supplies and authorizes that exact content for this record.
- Never overwrite, delete, or silently revise an existing entry. Require Chris to identify a revision target and approve the new text.

## Chronicle workflow

1. Define the scope and time horizon. Inventory the sources, their dates, and their evidence class. State any material coverage gap.
2. Draft the record using the structure below. Link or cite evidence closely to the claim it supports. Keep reported and inferred material visibly labeled.
3. Verify internal consistency: decisions must identify their authority or remain proposed; results must distinguish observed from claimed; limitations must include evidence gaps that could change the conclusion.
4. Before saving, present the proposed entry and exact target directory. On Chris's explicit approval, create one new Markdown file named `YYYY-MM-DD-research-chronicle.md`, using the next available `-NN` suffix if needed.
5. Report the saved path, evidence coverage, and unresolved questions. Do not claim a release, validation, or implementation merely because it was discussed or drafted.

## Entry structure

```markdown
# Research Chronicle: YYYY-MM-DD — [topic]

## Scope

## Evidence reviewed

## Decisions and rationale

## Results

## Limitations and open questions

## Next questions
```

Omit empty sections only when doing so makes the record more honest. Include source links or local paths where available. For research involving personal or proprietary material, minimize detail and preserve the authorization boundary in the entry.
