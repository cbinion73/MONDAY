# MONDAY Activity Ledger

Append to `/Users/chris.binion/Knowledge Vault/Monday Knowledge/100 Activity Ledger`. Record only observable authorized activity with time, classification, source, summary, artifacts, and limitations.

Use the plugin-root `scripts/monday_system.py record-activity` command. Never claim the ledger is a complete record of Chris's day. Do not include secrets, raw private communications, personal-journal narrative, or hidden reasoning.

Corrections append a superseding receipt; they do not silently alter history. Read [the ledger contract](references/activity-ledger-contract.md) before adding a new activity classification.
