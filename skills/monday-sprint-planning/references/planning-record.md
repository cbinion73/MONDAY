# Sprint planning record

Use this as a compact decision record. Omit a field only when it is not relevant; label required-but-unknown information plainly.

```markdown
# Sprint plan: [team] | [dates]

## Status
Draft | Proposed | Accepted

## Sprint goal
[One observable outcome and why it matters.]

## Evidence and planning basis
- Sources read: [backlog, delivery history, incident/support load, roadmap, etc.]
- Capacity basis: [people or team capacity, absences, allocation assumptions]
- Delivery signal: [historical range or explanation of why unavailable]
- Material assumptions: [assumptions that change feasibility]

## Committed work
| Work item | Outcome / acceptance evidence | Effort signal | Owner | Dependencies / risks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [item] | [done means] | [estimate or qualitative signal] | [person/team] | [none or detail] |

## Stretch or pull-in queue
| Rank | Work item | Why it is not committed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [item] | [capacity, readiness, dependency, etc.] |

## Excluded, unready, or blocked work
- [item]: [disposition and rationale]

## Decisions and checkpoints
| Decision, dependency, or risk | Owner | Next action / checkpoint | Consequence if unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [item] | [person/team] | [action/date] | [impact] |

## Acceptance
- Planning decision owner: [name or unknown]
- Team acceptance / reservations: [record or unknown]
- Remaining gaps and next action: [concise list]
```

Do not turn the record into a claim that the goal is guaranteed. It captures a bounded, evidence-informed commitment at a point in time.
