# MONDAY Operations

Write operational records under `/Users/chris.binion/Knowledge Vault/Monday Knowledge/400 MONDAY Operations`. These records explain what MONDAY attempted, what sources were used, what succeeded, what failed, and what remains unresolved.

Use the plugin-root `scripts/monday_system.py record-operation` command for receipts. Label observations, hypotheses, decisions, and open questions distinctly. Link authoritative records instead of duplicating their claims.

Never conceal corrections, source gaps, or partial runs. An operations receipt cannot itself prove that a project, Calendar change, message, or app display was completed.

Read [the operations contract](references/operations-contract.md) when adding a new receipt type or health field.
