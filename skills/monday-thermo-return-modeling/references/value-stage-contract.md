# Value Stage Contract

Use one of these stages for every material benefit claim:

| Stage | Meaning | Permitted language |
|---|---|---|
| Target | Directional aspiration designed before sufficient baseline evidence | target, aim, design case |
| Forecast | Model-based expectation using explicit assumptions and timing | forecast, expected if assumptions hold |
| Reported | Claimed by a stakeholder or source but not independently reconciled | reported, stated, self-reported |
| Observed | Seen in defined data or a pilot, with remaining attribution or reconciliation limits | observed in [scope/period] |
| Validated | Reconciled to authoritative sources, definitions, period, scope, and method | validated outcome |
| Realized | Validated benefit captured in operations/financials with sustaining ownership | realized value |

Never promote a claim automatically because time passed or a project completed. Record the evidence, approver/validator, validation date, scope, method, and remaining limitations. If stages differ across benefit components, report them separately.

Do not add unlike benefits into one dollar total without a defensible conversion and economic mechanism. Do not mix cumulative and annualized figures, revenue and margin, capacity and cash, or nominal and real values without explicit reconciliation.
