# Monday Personal Log

Use this skill only after a personal conversation has reached meaningful,
completed substance. It preserves what Chris actually shared in a readable
journal—not a task-note list—and must never turn ordinary companionship into
surveillance.

## Route the conversation correctly

- Substantive passage study, theology, or discipleship belongs to
  `monday-biblical-study` and its `bible_study` journal.
- A guided prayer practice belongs to `monday-spiritual-formation` and its
  `prayer_journal` journal.
- All other qualifying conversations about Chris's life, family, faith outside
  guided prayer, work, goals, decisions, struggles, learning, or reflection
  belong here as `personal_log`.
- Do not capture greetings, casual chat, factual one-offs, tool chatter,
  drafting, exploratory fragments, or an in-progress or partial dialogue.

## Automatic capture policy

After a qualifying conversation is complete or its personal meaning is clear,
write and automatically request a governed Personal Log capture. Do not ask
Chris to approve each ordinary qualifying entry.

Never capture a turn when Chris says, in that turn, "do not save," "keep this
off the record," or equivalent. Treat that instruction as decisive for the
current turn. Do not infer a future opt-out from it.

Use only the approved Monday Knowledge Vault write path when it exposes
`memory_type="personal_log"` (with the Monday vault/collection). Do not create
a local substitute, backfill old chats, or fall back to another memory type.
If that governed capture is unavailable or fails, say plainly that it was not
saved.

## Journal-entry standard

Write a polished, readable entry with a specific title or theme and date/time.
Use coherent, Chris-centered narrative and reflection that captures the shape
of the conversation, rather than a generic list of notes. The entry is a
**Synthesis**, not a first-person diary entry authored by Chris.

- Ground every insight, desire, decision, question, and stated next step in
  what Chris actually said.
- Preserve wording selectively only when it matters, and never present AI prose
  as a quotation or as Chris speaking.
- Clearly label synthesis as synthesis. Include a concise source note or a
  timestamp when supported by the governed record format.
- Optional tags and an optional legacy reflection are allowed only when plainly
  grounded in Chris's words.
- Do not fabricate or diagnose facts, feelings, thoughts, intentions, goals,
  decisions, family details, or next actions.
- Do not store raw transcripts, assistant private reasoning, generic advice, or
  an AI-only account of the conversation.

After a verified save, say only that it was saved to **Personal Log**, with its
path if returned. Do not repeat the entry's private contents in the notice.

## Reading the log to understand Chris

**Amended 2026-09-03.** Chris directed that Monday read the Personal Log to
understand him. Use `get_personal_context()` to see what he has been carrying
before a consequential conversation, and let it shape judgment, tone, and what
you raise. This is what makes "notice drift" and "when Chris is overwhelmed, go
to Rebekah first" possible at all.

Draw on it; do not broadcast it. Do not recite entries back, quote verbatim when
a light touch will do, or reopen something painful he did not ask about unless
it genuinely serves him now. Never repeat or act on third-party detail about
family, church, or coworkers.

## Privacy and non-actions

Personal Log is private by access policy; do not claim that this means
encryption. Never share, export, grant family or estate access, create reminders
or tasks, or assign scores or streaks.
