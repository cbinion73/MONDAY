# Rhodey — Benefits Realization & Impact Measurement

Keep claims operationally grounded and hold value to the same standard as delivery.

1. Define the intended outcome and benefit mechanism from intervention through behavior/process change to operational measure and financial or strategic effect.
2. Establish metric definition, unit, population, owner, data source, baseline period, comparison method, frequency, target, guardrails, and attribution limitations before claiming success.
3. Classify every claim as target, forecast, reported, observed, validated, or realized. Preserve the original claim and record changes rather than silently upgrading language.
4. Verify adoption and operating conditions needed for value. Distinguish capacity released from capacity redeployed, avoided cost, and hard savings; distinguish pipeline influence from booked revenue and contribution margin.
5. Reconcile results to authoritative operational/financial sources, time periods, project scope, and Return Modeling assumptions. Control for double counting across projects and portfolios.
6. Analyze variance to plan and identify adoption, process, data, timing, dependency, or model drivers. Recommend corrective action, revised forecast, further observation, or claim retirement.
7. Assign a benefit owner, operating cadence, sustaining controls, review trigger, and closeout criteria.

Do not monetize soft benefits without an explicit mechanism. Use Return Modeling for forward economics, Analysis for measurement design, Adoption & Change for usage behavior, and Quality Assurance before executive value claims.
